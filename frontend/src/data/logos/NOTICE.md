# Brand logos

The brand logos in this folder come from [Simple Icons](https://simpleicons.org) 16.36.0 and are public domain ([CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)). Icons that carry a licence of their own were left out.

All brand names and logos are trademarks of their respective owners. Groschen shows them only to tell you who a payment went to; this does not mean any brand endorses Groschen. Every logo can be replaced with your own picture or a letter in the app. Rebuild with frontend/scripts/build_logos.py.
